Who We Are

Founded in 2018, Ozone Amanzi began with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to clean, safe drinking water. What started as a small local delivery service has grown into a trusted name across Cape Town, serving thousands of homes, offices, and communities.

Our name, Amanzi — the Nguni word for water — reflects our deep connection to the people and places we serve. We combine advanced ozone purification technology with a commitment to sustainability, ensuring that every drop we deliver is as pure as nature intended.

We are proud to be a 100% South African company, driven by a team that cares about quality, reliability, and the well-being of our customers.
